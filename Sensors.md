Practically any medical sensor that stores, processes, or transmits data (wired, wireless, or via a connected device) can have cybersecurity measures applied. A truly exhaustive list isn't possible because new devices keep appearing, but these are the main categories.

**Implantable sensors**
- Pacemakers and ICD/CRT cardiac devices (including leadless pacemakers)
- Implantable loop recorders and cardiac monitors
- Continuous glucose monitor (CGM) implants (e.g., Eversense)
- Neurostimulator sensors (deep brain stimulation, spinal cord stimulation, vagus nerve stimulation)
- Implantable pressure sensors (e.g., CardioMEMS for heart failure, intracranial and intraocular pressure)
- Cochlear implant sensors and sensing components of retinal implants
- Insulin pump sensing components

**Wearable and body-worn sensors**
- Smartwatch and fitness-band sensors (heart rate, ECG, SpO₂, temperature, accelerometer)
- Wearable CGMs (Dexcom, FreeStyle Libre)
- Holter monitors and ECG patches
- Wearable blood pressure monitors
- Smart rings and sleep trackers
- Fall-detection and activity sensors
- Smart textiles and e-skin sensors
- EEG headsets and EMG sensors
- Wearable defibrillators

**Bedside and clinical monitoring sensors**
- ECG/EKG electrodes and leads
- Pulse oximeters
- Non-invasive and invasive blood pressure sensors
- Capnography (CO₂) sensors
- Temperature probes
- Respiratory flow and ventilator sensors
- Infusion pump flow, pressure, and occlusion sensors
- Fetal and maternal monitoring sensors
- EEG and intracranial pressure monitors
- Anesthesia gas sensors
- Neonatal and incubator sensors

**Imaging and diagnostic sensors**
- X-ray, CT, and MRI detectors and sensor arrays
- Ultrasound transducers
- PET/SPECT detectors
- Digital pathology scanners and sensors
- Endoscope and capsule endoscopy image sensors
- Retinal and OCT sensors

**Point-of-care and lab sensors**
- Blood glucose meters
- Blood gas and electrolyte analyzers
- Coagulation (INR) sensors
- Lab-on-chip and biosensor cartridges
- Smart inhalers and spirometers
- Connected thermometers and home blood pressure cuffs

**Remote patient monitoring and home health sensors**
- Connected scales, pill dispensers, and medication adherence sensors
- Home sleep apnea and CPAP sensors
- Personal emergency response and ambient assisted-living sensors (motion, bed, door, and presence sensors)
- Smart home health hubs

**Hospital environment and asset sensors**
- RTLS/RFID/BLE tracking tags for equipment and patients
- Patient-bed occupancy and pressure sensors
- Environmental sensors (temperature, humidity, air quality, pressure in ORs and cleanrooms)
- Cold-chain sensors for vaccines, blood, and pharmaceuticals
- Smart IV pole and hand-hygiene compliance sensors

**Surgical and robotic sensors**
- Force and haptic sensors in surgical robots
- Surgical navigation and tracking sensors
- Intraoperative neuromonitoring sensors

**Emerging sensors**
- Ingestible sensors and smart pills
- Smart contact lenses
- Sweat and saliva biosensors
- Brain-computer interface electrodes
- Smart bandages and wound sensors

**Cybersecurity measures that apply across these**
- Encryption of data at rest and in transit (e.g., BLE, Wi-Fi, cellular links)
- Authentication and pairing security, and device identity (certificates)
- Secure boot and signed firmware updates
- Network segmentation and monitoring
- Hardware security modules and secure elements
- Sensor data integrity checks and anti-spoofing or anti-tampering protections
- Logging, vulnerability management, and software bills of materials (SBOMs)
- Privacy controls (HIPAA/GDPR compliance) and secure cloud and app backends

Relevant frameworks include FDA premarket and postmarket cybersecurity guidance, the EU MDR/IVDR with MDCG 2019-16, IEC 81001-5-1, IEC 62443, and AAMI TIR57.
